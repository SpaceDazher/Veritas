# Veritas — promo plan

**Angle:** a research verdict is not a production permission — Veritas makes every claim carry its receipt.
**Hook:** `Every claim needs a receipt.` — one line, no context, works as a thumbnail; the counter lands immediately after.
**Why this project:** the honest tension is the product. The commit history is not features, it is `evidence: reseal…`, `chore: refreeze the integrity manifest…`, `rebind the closure record`. A platform whose own history is the audit trail, whose CLI prints `"mode": "PUBLIC_SYNTHETIC_DEMO"` before you ask, and whose README refuses to call itself production-ready.
**Tone:** dead-serious-founder · **Palette:** `paper` (the product's own cream + deep green + brick) · **Music:** `minimal` — sub bass and bells, no drums @ 96 bpm, root 40
**Length:** 20.0 s + 0.8 s brand card · **Format:** landscape 1080p30 · **Loop:** yes
